## 1. Day 15 Objective

The objective of Day 15 is to demonstrate the complete
SolarGuard AI prototype.

The demonstration covers solar energy forecasting,
maintenance assistance, fault diagnosis, maintenance
guidance, and maintenance scheduling.

---

## 2. Final System Features

The SolarGuard AI prototype provides:

- Solar energy output forecasting
- Weather parameter analysis
- Maintenance recommendation
- Maintenance knowledge retrieval
- Fault diagnosis
- Technician maintenance guidance
- Maintenance scheduling
- Prediction analysis
- Web-based user interface

---

## 3. Demonstration Workflow

### Step 1 – Energy Forecasting

Enter the following weather parameters:

- Temperature
- Humidity
- Solar irradiance
- Cloud cover
- Wind speed
- Panel temperature

Click:

`Predict Energy`

The system displays the predicted solar energy output
and maintenance status.

---

### Step 2 – Maintenance Assistant

Enter a technician question such as:

`My solar output is low.`

Click:

`Ask Assistant`

The system identifies the relevant maintenance issue
and provides maintenance guidance.

---

### Step 3 – Fault Diagnosis

Enter:

`My inverter is showing a warning.`

The system provides:

- Identified issue
- Recommended maintenance steps
- Safety guidance

---

### Step 4 – Maintenance Scheduling

Enter:

Maintenance Activity:

`Panel Cleaning`

Maintenance Date:

`2026-10-10`

Click:

`Schedule Maintenance`

The system displays a scheduling confirmation.

---

### Step 5 – Prediction Analysis

Click:

`View Prediction Analysis`

The system displays the solar energy prediction analysis.

---
